Fail only the world that overflows its quota of the contact lists in ``SolverIPC`` with independent worlds, instead of every world: through libuipc-warp's capacity per world every world holds a quota of the contact lists the worlds share, sized as for that world alone, and a world whose contact asks for more fails alone, back at its state at the step's start, while the other worlds keep every contact pair and take their step. An eager step grows that world's quota and solves the step again; a captured step sets ``OVERFLOW`` in that world's ``STATUS`` entry alone, and ``grow()`` (or ``check_status(..., grow=True)``) grows its quota, so a caller that grows on ``OVERFLOW`` reads every world's entry. A step in which a world failed now writes the contact rows of the other worlds, and a coupled solver's proxies in those worlds get their feedback.
