``SolverIPC`` now moves a kinematic body with mass and colliding shapes inside the step toward the pose that ``state_in`` prescribes (``kinematic_motion="aim"``), so that contact and friction act on its motion; before, it jumped to that pose before the step. Pass ``kinematic_motion="jump"`` for the jump, which ``contact_constitution="al-ipc"`` needs, and so does a kinematic body of the global world with independent worlds.
