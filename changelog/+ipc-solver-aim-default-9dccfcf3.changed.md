``SolverIPC``'s ``kinematic_motion`` now defaults to ``None``, which moves a kinematic body with mass and colliding shapes inside the step toward the pose that ``state_in`` prescribes (``"aim"``) on the barrier pipeline when every such body can aim, so that contact and friction act on its motion. Otherwise it takes the swept jump (under AL-IPC, for such a body of the global world with independent worlds, or for one with a singular inertia), and ``SolverIPC.kinematic_motion`` gives the choice.
