Start ``SolverIPC``'s BDF2 history anew where gravity changes: with ``integrator="bdf2"`` the step after ``notify_model_changed`` took a new gravity is an implicit Euler step of the worlds whose gravity changed, decided on the device by comparing with the step before's gravity, also inside a captured graph, so that the velocity follows the new gravity at once instead of lagging half a step's change of velocity behind it for good. External forces still change within a history.
