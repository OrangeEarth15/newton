Add ``kinematic_motion`` and ``kinematic_strength_ratio`` to ``SolverIPC``. With ``kinematic_motion="aim"`` a kinematic body with colliding shapes is an unknown of the step, pulled by libuipc's soft transform constraint toward the pose of ``state_in`` advanced by its twist over the step, with its velocity set so that the step's prediction is that pose: collision detection sweeps its motion, friction sees it, and a body that contact blocks stops short of its aim, lagging it under a load ``F`` by about ``F * dt**2 / ((1 + kinematic_strength_ratio) * m)``. ``state_out`` holds the pose it reached and the twist of ``state_in``, which it passes through; the first step and the step after ``reset`` take ``state_in``. Shapes that are each kinematic or static do not collide in this mode. The default, ``"jump"``, keeps the behaviour of before: the pose of ``state_in`` is taken before the step, and the jump is not swept. Not with ``contact_constitution="al-ipc"``.
