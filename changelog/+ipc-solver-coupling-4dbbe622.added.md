Add ``CouplingInterface`` to ``SolverIPC``, so that it can be an entry of Newton's coupled solvers (``SolverCoupled``, ``SolverCoupledProxy``). On an entry's view it takes a body another entry owns as kinematic and leaves that entry's cloth, soft bodies and springs out; proxy bodies and particles are unknowns that collide with neither another proxy nor a static shape, and the coupler's write of a proxy's state keeps the friction of its contacts. A coupler that solves a step more than once (``SolverCoupledProxy`` or ``SolverCoupledADMM`` with more than one iteration) is refused, and so are proxies with ``integrator="bdf2"`` or ``contact_constitution="al-ipc"``. ``check_status()`` without a container reports the status bits an entry kept since the last call.
