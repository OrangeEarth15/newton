Remove ``enable_d6_joints``, ``enable_distance_joints`` and ``enable_joint_armature`` from ``SolverIPC``: the D6 joints that lock what one of libuipc's joints locks, distance joints of one length or none, and the armature of revolute and prismatic joints are always simulated, where they were refused without the options. Drop the arguments; a model that ran with the options on runs as before.
