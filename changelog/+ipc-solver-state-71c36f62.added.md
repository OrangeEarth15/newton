Make ``SolverIPC`` read ``state_in`` on every step: a step that takes a new state for a dynamic particle or body discards the friction of its contacts, as ``SolverIPC.reset`` does, also inside a captured graph. A step that is not captured runs its solve as a CUDA graph of its own. ``SolverIPC.grow`` enlarges contact lists after a captured step reported an overflow, and ``notify_model_changed`` updates gravity and body mass and inertia in place, so captured graphs take them.
