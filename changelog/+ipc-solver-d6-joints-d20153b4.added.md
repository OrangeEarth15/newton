Add ``enable_d6_joints`` to ``SolverIPC``. With it a D6 joint that locks what one of libuipc's joints locks is simulated as that joint: no axis as a fixed joint, one angular axis as a revolute joint and one linear axis as a prismatic joint, with their drives, springs, damping, limits and ``joint_f``, three orthonormal angular axes as a ball joint, and three orthonormal linear and three orthonormal angular axes as no joint. ``joint_q`` and ``joint_qd`` hold Newton's coordinates of the D6 joint, and ``joint_f`` is Featherstone's generalized force. Other D6 joints, which lock what no libuipc joint locks, are refused, naming what they lock.
