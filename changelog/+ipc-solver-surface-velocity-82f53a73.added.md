Take collision meshes with ``Mesh.enable_surface_velocity`` in ``SolverIPC``, which refused them: friction drags what touches such a mesh with the velocities of its finalized Warp mesh, scaled and turned into the world by the shape's pose at the step's start, as Newton's other solvers take them. Only the part along a contact's tangent plane acts. ``contact_constitution="al-ipc"`` refuses such a mesh.
