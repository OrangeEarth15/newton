Make ``SolverIPC`` integrate the rotation of its rigid bodies as a rigid body's by default: ``body_rotation`` now defaults to ``None``, which is ``"rigid"`` under ``integrator="bdf1"`` and ``"linear"``, libuipc's step, under ``integrator="bdf2"``, which the rigid flight does not take (``body_rotation="rigid"`` with ``integrator="bdf2"`` raises ``ValueError``). In libuipc's step a free spin loses about ``(omega dt)^2`` of its angular velocity per step and a body's twist holds the skew part of ``A_dot A^-1``; by default a free body now keeps its angular momentum and kinetic energy, and its twist holds the angular velocity that carries its angular momentum. Contact scenes take other trajectories than in libuipc's step wherever bodies turn. The default needs libuipc-warp's ``affine_rotation`` (libuipc-warp 0.4.4); pass ``body_rotation="linear"`` to keep libuipc's step, also with an older libuipc-warp.
