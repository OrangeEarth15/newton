Add ``contact_damping`` to ``SolverIPC``: a viscous force against the motion of surfaces in contact along their normal, so that impacts lose energy under BDF2 or small steps, where IPC's barrier otherwise gives an impact back. ``None`` (the default) adds none, a number is every contact pair's coefficient in N s/m, and ``"material"`` takes ``shape_material_kd`` and ``soft_contact_kd`` as ``SolverVBD`` combines them.
