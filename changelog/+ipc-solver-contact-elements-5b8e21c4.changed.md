Make ``SolverIPC`` collide the pairs that Newton's collision groups, worlds, collide flags and filter pairs let collide, with the friction of the two materials, instead of refusing models with collision groups or filter pairs and giving every contact one coefficient. Two shapes collide as ``Model.shape_contact_pairs`` lists them (checked at construction), a particle with the shapes that have ``ShapeFlags.COLLIDE_PARTICLES``, free particles with each other when Newton's particle solvers collide them, and an inactive particle with nothing. The friction of two shapes is the arithmetic mean of their ``shape_material_mu``, of a particle and a shape the mean of ``soft_contact_mu`` and the shape's, and of two particles ``particle_mu``. A model whose friction came from the solver's ``friction_mu`` default (0.5) now takes Newton's default materials (shape ``mu`` 1.0, ``soft_contact_mu`` 0.5, ``particle_mu`` 0.5); set those to keep 0.5.
