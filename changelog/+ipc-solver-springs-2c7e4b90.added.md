Add springs to ``SolverIPC``: Newton's springs become libuipc's Hookean springs with the energy ``0.5 * ke * (l - L0)**2`` of ``spring_stiffness`` and ``spring_rest_length``, and the particles they join keep their contact. A spring with damping (libuipc has no damping element), a rest length of 0 or a negative stiffness raises ``NotImplementedError``; ``spring_control`` is not read, as by Newton's other solvers.
