Simulate a model with particles, bodies or colliding shapes in several worlds in ``SolverIPC`` as independent worlds, instead of refusing it unless ``coupled_worlds=True``: through libuipc-warp's independent worlds every world takes the step it would take alone, with its own Newton iterations, line searches, convergence test, linear solves and status, and a world that fails is put back at its state at the step's start while the other worlds take their step. ``STATUS`` and ``SOLVER_RESIDUAL`` hold every world's own values, a step that is not captured and fails in some worlds writes ``state_out`` and raises naming them, ``check_status`` names them, a coupled solver's entry keeps the status bits of every world, and the global entry of ``reset``'s world mask selects nothing. Refused with independent worlds, naming ``coupled_worlds=True``: a free particle, a dynamic body or proxy of the global world and, with ``kinematic_motion="aim"``, a global kinematic body with colliding shapes; a triangle, tetrahedron or spring between two worlds. ``coupled_worlds=True`` solves the worlds as one, as before. Needs libuipc-warp's independent worlds (``ArrayScene(independent_subscenes=True)``, ``write_frame_status(worlds=)``), past its 0.4.2 release.
