Let ``SolverIPC`` take a coupler that solves a step more than once (``SolverCoupledProxy`` or ``SolverCoupledADMM`` with more than one iteration): every pass after the first restarts from the step's start, which the solver keeps on the device (libuipc-warp's frame restart: the float64 positions and velocities, the BDF2 history, the active contact set the step takes its friction from, the step's requests, the joints' angles; and what the solver carries from step to step), saved by the step after the coupler's write of the input state and restored by the step after a restart's notification, inside a captured graph too. A pass with the inputs of the one before repeats it, bit for bit where a step repeats itself, with independent worlds in every world; the status bits the solver keeps for ``check_status()`` are a coupled step's last pass's. With several passes, ``SolverCoupledProxy``'s lagged transfer brings a proxy resting on the solver's content to rest; its staggered transfer needs a relaxation (``proxy_relaxation=0.5``), as each pass's feedback undoes the motion that made it.
