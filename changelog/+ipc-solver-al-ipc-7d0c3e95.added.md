Add ``contact_constitution`` to ``SolverIPC``: ``"al-ipc"`` takes libuipc's augmented-Lagrangian contact, whose constraint keeps contact at ``d_hat`` beyond the contact thickness, in place of the barrier (``"ipc"``, the default). It uses no barrier stiffness (``adaptive_kappa`` is refused) and has no Newton residual (``SOLVER_RESIDUAL`` is not offered). Its constraints hold between surfaces of vertices with mass, so models that collide particles on no cloth or tetrahedron, static mesh shapes, or particles and bodies without mass are refused; static planes are taken. New body masses build the solve again.
