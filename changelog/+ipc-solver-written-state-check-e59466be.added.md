Check the positions `SolverIPC` takes from `state_in` between steps, as the first step's start is checked: where a step takes a new position for a dynamic body or particle, as after a reset, surfaces through each other or at or inside their contact thickness fail the step with `StatusBits.THICKNESS_VIOLATED` before it runs, on the device and inside a captured graph too; a step that is not captured raises `RuntimeError` naming the pair. The state is taken as written, with no sweep. A step that writes nothing costs a conditional node and the reset of two small arrays of flags beside the comparison its loads already make, 12 µs on a step of 0.49 ms of 64 boxes; one that writes a box among them, 0.18 ms more.
