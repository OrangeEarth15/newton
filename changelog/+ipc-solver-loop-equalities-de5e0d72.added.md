Take the MuJoCo equality rows that Newton's MJCF and USD importers turn into loop joints in SolverIPC, by SolverSteel's rule: a CONNECT row linked to a BALL joint and a WELD row linked to a FIXED joint (a BALL joint for a torquescale of 0) in no articulation, between the row's bodies, is held by that joint, which the solver simulates. Models imported with the default convert_mjc_equality_constraints=True were refused for those rows; other enabled rows are still refused.
