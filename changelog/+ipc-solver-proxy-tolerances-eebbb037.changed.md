As an entry of a coupled solver with proxies, ``SolverIPC`` now takes converged steps by default: ``newton_velocity_tol`` 5e-4 m/s, ``newton_transrate_tol`` 1e-3 1/s and ``semi_implicit=False``, where an entry without proxies keeps 0.05 m/s, 0.1 1/s and ``True``. A proxy's feedback is the contact force at the end of the step, and at the old defaults an impact's feedback was up to 27 times the converged one; now it is within 1% in rigid scenes, at about twice the Newton iterations.
