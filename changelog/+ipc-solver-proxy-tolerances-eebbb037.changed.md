Make ``SolverIPC`` take converged steps by default as an entry of a coupled solver with proxies: ``newton_velocity_tol``, ``newton_transrate_tol`` and ``semi_implicit`` now default to ``None``, which takes 5e-4 m/s, 1e-3 1/s and ``False`` with proxies and libuipc's 0.05 m/s, 0.1 1/s and ``True`` otherwise. A proxy's feedback is the force of the step's contact terms at its end, and at the old defaults an impact step's feedback was up to 27 times the converged one; with these it stays within 1% of it in the measured scenes, at about twice the Newton iterations. Pass the arguments to take other values.
