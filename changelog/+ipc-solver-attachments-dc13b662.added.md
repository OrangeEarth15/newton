Add particle-body attachments and springs of rest length 0 to ``SolverIPC``: each becomes libuipc's soft vertex stitch, a term ``0.5 * k * dt**2 * |x_p - x_a|**2`` of the step's incremental potential, the spring ``0.5 * k * |x_p - x_a|**2`` of implicit Euler, between the particle and an anchor of the body at ``attachment_particle_body_body_point`` (a point that moves with the body and is no contact primitive), or between the spring's two particles. The stiffness and ``attachment_particle_body_enabled`` are read on the device at every step, so a captured step takes new values at its next replay. A hard attachment (infinite stiffness) takes the penalty of SolverIPC's ball joint, ``joint_strength_ratio`` times the dynamic masses it joins. Attachment damping raises ``NotImplementedError``, as spring damping does: libuipc's stitch has no damping.
