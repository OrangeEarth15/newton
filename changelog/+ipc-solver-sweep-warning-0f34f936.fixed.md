Fix ``SolverIPC`` printing ``OptionWarning: option 'sweep_kinematic' has no effect without contact (nothing to sweep against)`` for a model in which nothing can collide, such as an articulation whose shapes all have collisions turned off: the solver asked libuipc-warp to sweep the moves of kinematic bodies and particles against contact surfaces even when the model had none. It now asks for the sweep only when the model has something that collides (particles, cloth, tetrahedra, or a colliding shape other than a plane); results do not change, since libuipc-warp never swept without contact surfaces.
