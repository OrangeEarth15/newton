Add input checks, external forces and a step status to ``SolverIPC``. Construction raises ``NotImplementedError`` for springs, particle-body attachments, mimic and equality constraints, tendons, muscles, non-zero activations, joints other than free joints and drive targets on them, ``requires_grad``, content in more than one world, contact filters, and the CPU device. ``State.particle_f``, ``State.body_f`` and the ``Control.joint_f`` of free joints act on the step. ``SolverIPC.ObservableKind.STATUS`` (a ``SolverIPC.StatusBits`` mask) and ``SOLVER_RESIDUAL`` report how a step ended, also in a captured graph, and a step that is not captured raises ``RuntimeError`` when it failed.
