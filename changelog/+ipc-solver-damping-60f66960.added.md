Apply Newton's damping in ``SolverIPC``, as Newton's VBD solver defines each coefficient: ``spring_damping``, ``attachment_particle_body_damping``, the tetrahedra's ``k_damp`` and the cloth's ``tri_kd`` and ``edge_kd``, which were refused or ignored before. Newton's default ``tri_kd`` of 10 now damps every cloth, and a negative or non-finite damping raises ``NotImplementedError``.
