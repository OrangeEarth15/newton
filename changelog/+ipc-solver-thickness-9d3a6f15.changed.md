Make ``SolverIPC`` take the contact thickness of a free particle from its ``particle_radius`` and that of a shape from its ``shape_margin`` (a static plane's surface moves out by it), so that a particle rests its radius above a shape, two particles the sum of their radii apart and two shapes the sum of their margins apart. The ``thickness`` argument now applies to the vertices of cloth and tetrahedra only, whose particle radius is not used: libuipc gives a vertex's thickness to every edge and triangle it is on. The check of the start at construction compares each pair of primitives with its own thickness and also tests free particles against each other, and needs ``SurfaceChecker.min_gap`` of libuipc-warp.
