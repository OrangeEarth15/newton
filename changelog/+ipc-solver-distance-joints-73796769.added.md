Add distance joints to ``SolverIPC``: a distance joint of one length (its two bounds equal and not negative) holds the origins of its joint frames that far apart as libuipc's soft vertex stitch of that rest length between anchors of the two bodies, with the penalty of a ball joint, and one with both bounds negative holds nothing; both take ``joint_q``, ``joint_qd`` and ``joint_f`` as a free joint does. A range of distances or a single bound, an inequality that a stitch cannot hold, is refused.
