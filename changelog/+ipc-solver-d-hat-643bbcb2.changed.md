Make ``SolverIPC`` choose a smaller ``d_hat`` for a model with small colliding features when no ``d_hat`` is given: libuipc's 0.01 m, at most a quarter of the smallest colliding shape's half-size and half the median edge length of the cloth and tetrahedral meshes. With 0.01 m a shape smaller than about 4 cm overflowed the contact lists of captured steps, and a cloth or soft body with edges shorter than about 2 cm rested stretched. Pass ``d_hat=0.01`` to keep the former value.
