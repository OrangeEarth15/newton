Turn ``SolverIPC``'s ``enable_joint_limit_damping`` and ``enable_joint_effort_limit`` on by default: ``joint_limit_kd`` damps a revolute or prismatic joint that starts a step past one of its limits, and a ``joint_effort_limit`` below 1e6 bounds a drive's force as decided at the start of every step, where it was refused. Pass ``enable_joint_limit_damping=False`` for libuipc's undamped limit, and ``enable_joint_effort_limit=False`` to refuse such limits.
