Check the installed ``libuipc-warp`` in ``SolverIPC`` by its release and the capabilities the solver calls: a libuipc-warp older than 0.4.4 (a development build counts as its release), or one without ``EngineOptions(affine_rotation=)``, ``EngineOptions(al_sweep_kinematic=)`` and the frame restart, raises ``ImportError`` naming what it lacks and where to install libuipc-warp from. The warning on libuipc-warp and Warp releases outside a tested range is gone, and ``body_rotation="linear"`` no longer makes an older libuipc-warp usable.
