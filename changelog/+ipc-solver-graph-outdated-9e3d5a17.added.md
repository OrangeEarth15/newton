Add ``SolverIPC.graph_outdated`` for callers that capture ``SolverIPC.step`` in a CUDA graph: the PCG loop of a captured step re-reads its stopping test every 8 or 32 iterations, fixed when the step is captured, and the steps now count their Newton and PCG iterations on the device; called after replays, ``graph_outdated`` returns ``True`` when they ask for the other length, or for other guesses of libuipc-warp's size ladders, which the engine then takes; the steps captured from then on take both. The graphs captured before stay valid. The ``cloth_ipc`` example captures again when it returns ``True``.
