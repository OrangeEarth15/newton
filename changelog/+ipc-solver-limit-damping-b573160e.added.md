Add ``enable_joint_limit_damping`` to ``SolverIPC``. With it a revolute or prismatic joint whose coordinate is past one of its limits at the start of a step is damped by ``joint_limit_kd``, as SolverFeatherstone and SolverSemiImplicit damp it: the force ``-joint_limit_kd * qd`` at the step's end velocity, folded into libuipc's driving term as ``joint_damping`` is, so that a body falling onto a limit no longer bounces off libuipc's undamped cubic limit. Without it ``joint_limit_kd`` is not read, as before.
