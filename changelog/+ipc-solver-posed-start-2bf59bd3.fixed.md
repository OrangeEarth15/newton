Check the start of ``SolverIPC``'s contact pairs with a kinematic body, or with a link that the model leaves at the identity pose, on the first step's ``state_in``, since the caller poses those bodies before the first step: a kinematic root whose model pose crossed another body, turned clear of it for the first step, was refused at construction. A first step captured in a graph, which cannot read its ``state_in``, raises when such a pair fails in the model's state. Pairs without such a body are checked at construction as before.
