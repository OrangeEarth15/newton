Add revolute, prismatic, fixed and ball joints to ``SolverIPC``. They become libuipc's affine-body joints between the bodies, or a body and the world: penalties of ``joint_strength_ratio`` (default 100, libuipc's) times the mass of the dynamic bodies they join. ``Control.joint_f`` acts on every joint, ``joint_enabled`` is read at every step, and the solver writes ``State.joint_q`` and ``State.joint_qd``, a revolute joint's angle going on past pi. Distance, D6 and rod joints, joint armature and joint friction raise ``NotImplementedError``.
