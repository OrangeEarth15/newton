Add ``body_rotation`` to ``SolverIPC``: ``"rigid"`` integrates the rotation of its rigid bodies as a rigid body's (libuipc-warp's ``affine_rotation="rigid"``), where libuipc's step (``"linear"``, the default) moves a body's 12 affine coordinates along a straight line and a free spin loses about ``(omega dt)^2`` of its angular velocity per step. A free body then keeps its angular momentum and kinetic energy, a spin about a principal axis is the exact motion, a torque spins a body up by exactly ``tau / I`` per second, and the body's twist holds the angular velocity that carries its angular momentum. Requires ``integrator="bdf1"``.
