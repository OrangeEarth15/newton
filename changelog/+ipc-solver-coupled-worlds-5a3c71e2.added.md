Add ``coupled_worlds`` to ``SolverIPC``: with it a model with particles, bodies or colliding shapes in more than one world is simulated instead of refused. Every world is a libuipc subscene of its own and the global entities one more that meets every world, so no contact pair joins two worlds, also where the worlds overlap in space as ``replicate`` places them, and global shapes act in every world. The worlds stay one solve: one step length, one line search, one Newton convergence test, one PCG and one set of contact lists, so a world's result depends on the other worlds through the iteration counts and step lengths, a failure in one world is reported in all of them, and a reset or a new state in one world discards the next step's friction in every world. Copies of one world take the iterations of one copy and follow its run to rounding. The engine takes at most 2048 contact elements times subscenes (the worlds and the global one); replicated worlds share their contact elements, those of shapes in filter pairs included. Without ``coupled_worlds`` such a model is still refused, and a model with one local world is solved as before.
