Add ``adaptive_kappa`` to ``SolverIPC``: with it the contact stiffness is taken as libuipc and its pyuipc front end take it, inside libuipc's scene-adaptive range, whose lower bound scales with the mean vertex mass and with 1/dt^2 and follows each step's ``dt``, also in captured steps. ``kappa=None`` then takes the lower bound, libuipc's default, and a given ``kappa`` is clamped into the range, the first step that clamps it warning with ``NewtonWarning``; ``SolverIPC.contact_stiffness(dt)`` gives the stiffness of a step of any ``dt``. By default ``kappa`` is taken as given, as before.
