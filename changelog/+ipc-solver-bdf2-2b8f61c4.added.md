Add ``integrator`` to ``SolverIPC``: ``"bdf2"`` takes libuipc's BDF2, second order, with less numerical damping than implicit Euler (``"bdf1"``, the default). The history of the scheme is the solver's: the first step, a step from a state the solver did not write last and the step after ``reset`` are implicit Euler steps, so that a step from a new state applies the whole impulse of its forces (a history started at rest, libuipc's start, gives two thirds of it and leaves the velocity half a step's impulse behind). Joint drives, passive springs and damping take BDF2's velocity at the end of the step.
