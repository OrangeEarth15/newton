Add the experimental `newton.solvers.SolverIPC`, an Incremental Potential Contact solver that runs on the optional `libuipc-warp` package (0.5.0 or newer) and never lets a surface pass through another: each step is one implicit time step in which a barrier and continuous collision detection keep surfaces apart. The step runs on the device and can be captured in a CUDA graph. It simulates:

  - Particles, tetrahedral soft bodies (stable Neo-Hookean), cloth (Baraff-Witkin membrane with discrete-shell bending and self-contact), springs and particle-body attachments, with Newton's damping coefficients as `SolverVBD` defines them and the drag and lift of triangles as `SolverSemiImplicit` computes them.
  - Rigid bodies as near-rigid affine bodies with box, sphere, capsule, cylinder, cone, ellipsoid and mesh shapes, whose rotation is integrated as a rigid body's by default (`body_rotation`); static shapes, planes and height fields.
  - Contact with lagged friction between the pairs that Newton's collision groups, worlds, collide flags and filter pairs let collide, with the mean friction of the two materials, shape margins and particle radii as contact thickness, a default `d_hat` of every shape and particle, as libuipc's per-geometry `d_hat`, below the small features it can touch, optional contact damping (`contact_damping`) and the surface velocity of collision meshes.
  - Revolute, prismatic, fixed, ball, D6, universal and distance joints as penalty energies, with drives, springs, damping, limits, armature, effort limits, joint friction and `Control.joint_f`; mimic joints and MuJoCo's fixed tendons; `joint_q` and `joint_qd` written after every step.
  - Rod joints, which `ModelBuilder.add_rod` and `add_joint_rod` make, as libuipc-warp's rod joints with Newton's strain: the stretch and shear of the child's anchor, the discrete elastic rod's bend and twist, and their damping, from `joint_target_ke`, `joint_target_kd` and `joint_rest_q`, with `SolverVBD`'s Dahl hysteresis where `model.vbd.dahl_eps_max` and `model.vbd.dahl_tau` are both above 0, which `reset` starts anew. A rod joint's `joint_q` and `joint_qd` are written as a free joint's, and its `joint_f` is a world wrench at the child's centre of mass, as in `SolverVBD`.
  - MuJoCo's spatial tendons, as libuipc-warp's spatial tendons: a path through sites, around spheres and cylinders (on a side site's side) and over pulleys, whose length and moment arms are MuJoCo's, with the spring, damping, armature, friction loss and limit of a fixed tendon, and the limit's stiffness from `solreflimit` through the tendon's inverse weight.
  - Kinematic particles and bodies, which move toward the poses of `state_in` inside the step or jump to them after a swept check (`kinematic_motion`).
  - Several worlds, each solved as it would be alone, or as one solve with `coupled_worlds=True`.
  - libuipc's BDF2 (`integrator="bdf2"`), augmented-Lagrangian contact (`contact_constitution="al-ipc"`) and MAS preconditioner (`fem_preconditioner="mas"`) as options.
  - The observables `STATUS`, `SOLVER_RESIDUAL`, `CONTACT_F`, `BODY_PARENT_F` and `BODY_QDD`, with `check_status()`, `grow()` and `graph_outdated()` for captured steps, `frame_stats()` for the counters of the last step and `contact_stiffness(dt)` for every world's contact stiffness. `StatusBits` say how a step ended: which failure stopped it, or which limit it reached (`NEWTON_LIMIT`, `LINE_SEARCH_LIMIT`, `PCG_LIMIT`), which does not fail it.
  - An entry of Newton's coupled solvers, proxies included.

  Positions that a step takes from `state_in` between steps, as after a reset, are checked as the first step's start is: surfaces through each other or at or inside their contact thickness fail the step with `StatusBits.THICKNESS_VIOLATED` before it runs, also inside a captured graph, and a step that is not captured raises `RuntimeError` naming the pair. It refuses at construction what it does not simulate, naming the item and what to change, among them the inputs of a rod joint it would not read (joint friction, armature, limits, `joint_stiffness`, `joint_damping`, target modes, a `joint_effort_limit` below 1e6), `SolverVBD`'s Dahl attributes on other joints, MuJoCo's inside wrap of a spatial tendon, and Newton's own routed tendons; it runs on CUDA devices only. The `cloth_ipc` example shows it, and twelve other examples run with it through `--solver ipc`.
