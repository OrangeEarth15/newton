Fix ``SolverIPC`` starting a cloth built at rest strained by the float32 rounding of ``Model.tri_poses`` (up to 6e-8), from which it took each triangle's rest shape. A triangle whose ``tri_poses`` are the float32 rounding of its rest shape at ``particle_q``, as Newton's builders compute it, now takes that shape in float64, and when every triangle does, the engine takes the rest shapes from the start positions, as libuipc takes them from its rest geometry.
