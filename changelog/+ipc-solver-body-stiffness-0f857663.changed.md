Make ``SolverIPC`` lower the orthogonality stiffness of a rigid body for a step in which libuipc's 1e8 Pa would keep the first Newton iteration from turning the body by 0.1 rad: ``body_kappa`` now defaults to ``None``, which takes 1e8 Pa or 25 times the body's largest principal moment of inertia over its volume and the squared step, whichever is lower, written before every step, also inside a captured graph. With 1e8 Pa the line search cut a turn short and the Newton loop's displacement test then ended the step: a 1 m cube that a torque should turn by 0.06 rad in a 0.1 s step reached 31% of its angular velocity, and a 10 cm cube spinning at 0.6 rad/s kept 33% of it over six steps of 1/60 s. The lower stiffness costs rigidity in proportion to the squared step: a strain of 4e-5 for a 10 cm cube under four others at 1/600 s and 4e-3 at 1/60 s. Pass ``body_kappa=1e8`` to keep the former value.
