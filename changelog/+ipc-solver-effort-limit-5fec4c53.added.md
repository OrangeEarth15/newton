Add ``enable_joint_effort_limit`` to ``SolverIPC``, on by default: a ``joint_effort_limit`` below Newton's default 1e6 bounds the force of a revolute or prismatic joint's drive as decided at the start of every step: a step whose drive force ``ke * (q_target - q) + kd * (qd_target - qd)`` exceeds the limit at its start takes the limit, with that sign, as a constant joint torque or force in place of the drive, whose passive spring and damping remain. libuipc's driving term is a quadratic potential that cannot bound its force, so a step that crosses the bound takes it one step late. With ``False`` such a limit on a driven degree of freedom is refused.
