Add joint armature to ``SolverIPC``: the ``joint_armature`` of a revolute or prismatic joint is the inertia of its coordinate, as SolverFeatherstone and SolverMuJoCo add it to the joint's inertia: the kinetic term ``a / (2 dt**2) * (q - q_pred)**2`` of the step, with the integrator's prediction of the coordinate from its rate at the step's start (BDF2 included), folded into libuipc's driving term with the drive, the passive spring and the damping. The armature of a ball or free joint is refused.
